"Your privacy matters. Here is a clear guide explaining what information we collect, who can see it, and how you can delete it at any time."

01. WHO WE ARE

Mirzam is a premium digital wedding and engagement invitation service. This page explains what information we keep, why we need it, and how you can control it.

For any inquiries, please message us on Instagram.

02. INFORMATION YOU SHARE WITH US

  • Account Data: Your name, email, or phone number. Your password is fully encrypted, meaning neither we nor anyone else can ever see it.
  • Invitation Content: All the beautiful details you add to your card — like your names, event date and location, photos, personal messages, and background music.
  • Contact Data: We handle most of our communication via Instagram, but you can provide an optional phone number if you prefer us to reach you there. We will never use this number for any other purpose.
  • Proof of Payment: A screenshot of your transfer receipt and wallet number or Instapay handle to confirm payment.
  • Automatic Security Data: When using our site, our systems automatically log basic technical details like your device type and IP address. We use this strictly to keep your account secure.

03. YOUR GUESTS' DATA

When your guests open your invitation, they may submit their names, attendance status (including number of companions), and a written message for your guestbook. They can also upload photos and record voice messages.

This data is entirely yours. We do not sell it, share it with anyone, or use it for marketing. However, we may occasionally contact you to ask for permission to showcase your invitation as a live example for future clients.

You have full administrative control, meaning you can easily delete or manage any messages, photos, or voice recordings that appear on your invitation.

04. ADDITIONAL SERVICES

To keep our site running smoothly and securely, we use the following trusted services:

  • Firebase (by Google): Used to securely store your invitation data and manage user accounts.
  • Cloudinary: Used to host and store the images and voice messages you upload.
  • GitHub: Used to host our website files online.

These services operate under their own strict privacy and security policies.

05. DATA RETENTION

  • Astra Plan: Your invitation link remains active online for one full year from activation.
  • Aurora Plan: Your invitation link remains available for a lifetime.
  • Free Trial: The preview link expires after 24 hours, but your saved design remains in your account.

Payment receipts are kept securely for accounting and verification purposes.

You can ask us to permanently delete your account at any time — just send us a message on Instagram.

06. YOUR DATA RIGHTS & SECURITY

You have the right to request a copy of your data, correct any mistakes, or completely delete your account by contacting us via Instagram.

We take your privacy seriously and use reliable, modern security measures to protect your account and data. Access to your information is strictly limited to our team when you require support.

While no digital system is 100% perfect, we are committed to keeping your memories safe and will always inform you if any issue arises.
